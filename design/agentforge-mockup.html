Design system

Logo — three directions

Replaces the generic gradient sparkle. The metaphor is a forge: agents shaping raw requirements into shipped code. Ember is the only brand color; it also marks live, “in the forge” work across the app.

Color — ember + graphite, de‑rainbowed status

Neutral graphite UI with an ink primary (restraint, not the generic AI indigo). A single ember accent carries identity and “active work”. Status collapses from seven hues to four meanings — every one paired with an icon and label, never color alone.
